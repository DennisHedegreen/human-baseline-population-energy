# Source attribution and scope

Reviewed 12 October 2026.

Source: Eurostat, DEMO_PJAN, Population on 1 January by age and sex, 2025;
captured 5 October 2026.

- [Dataset](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/demo_pjan/default/table)
- [Statistical reuse policy](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice)

Eurostat authorises commercial and non-commercial reuse of statistical data
with acknowledgement, subject to its stated exceptions and individual notices.
This EU27 population capture is not trade data or a non-EU-country collection.
The policy was checked for this edition; no blanket CC BY label is assigned to
the statistical files merely because Eurostat editorial content uses that licence.

Hedegreen Research selected the countries/year and reshaped the source response.
Own labels, translations, formatting and calculations. Original statistical
values are preserved; derived totals and scenarios are not official Eurostat
statistics. Eurostat is not responsible for this app, its adaptations or results.
The original response and manifest permit inspection of the capture and flags.

The kcal value is user input. This public edition contains no NNR transcription,
WHO growth reference, birth-flow model or pregnancy/breastfeeding allowance.
Their separate research-source reviews do not apply to this payload. Their
omission is a scoped model choice, not a declaration that those facts are forbidden.

The project code has no newly granted blanket licence. Dependency licences
remain with their respective packages; requirements refer to packages rather
than copying their source into this repository. No logos or credentials included.
